# SmartySDR for Android

**Your FlexRadio station and logbook, in your hand.**

SmartySDR combines live radio control, a panadapter and waterfall, voice operation, CW decoding and macros, FT digital modes, and a portable logbook in one Android app. Connect to your compatible FlexRadio locally or through SmartLink—or open the logbook on its own while operating another radio.

## Radio operation

- **Local and SmartLink connections:** discover radios on your network or connect remotely using your SmartLink account. A low-bandwidth option reduces streaming demands.
- **Live spectrum and waterfall:** tune by touch or tuning wheel, enter a frequency manually, select tuning steps, and adjust display range, colour gain and refresh rate.
- **Band, mode and antenna controls:** select bands and operating modes, choose RX/TX antennas, and recall saved operating settings.
- **Receiver controls:** filter bandwidth, AGC, noise blanker/reduction, automatic notch, WNB, RIT/XIT and RF/AF controls, subject to radio support.
- **Transmit controls:** PTT/MOX, RF power, microphone selection and gain, processor, monitor, VOX and antenna tuner controls. Live RX/TX status and power information help you follow operation.
- **Audio routing:** automatic or manually selected phone, wired, USB and Bluetooth outputs. Device behaviour depends on Android and the connected audio profile.
- **FlexControl:** USB OTG knob control and configurable button assignments.

## CW and FT modes

- **CW workspace:
- ** decoded text, adjustable text size, pitch and WPM settings, squelch and filtering controls, signal tuning, and selectable callsigns for logging.
- **Separate macros:
- ** CW and voice have independent macro banks. CW macros can insert the selected DX callsign.
- ** Fully working FT8,FT4 and FT2 modes:
- ** integrated receive/transmit workspace, band/mode frequencies, decoded station selection, CQ and QSO sequencing, halt controls, UTC checks and DAX diagnostics.
- **Integrated logging:** save contacts from operating screens and enrich them through configured QRZ services.

## One logbook, at home or portable

- Open the logbook without connecting to a radio.
- Enter callsign, name, QTH, frequency, mode and reports; save with current UTC date/time.
- Select a band to suggest a frequency for the current mode, or enter the actual frequency manually.
- View station photographs and a location map when available from the configured lookup service.
- Browse and edit Recent QSOs; import, export and share ADIF logs.
- Use separate default notes for portable operation and connected-radio operation, while keeping contacts in one logbook.
- Configure QRZ lookup and automatic or manual uploads. Lookup and upload credentials/subscriptions are separate QRZ services.
- Connect to a DX cluster, filter spots by band/mode, select a spotted station to fill the log entry, and submit spots manually. The app distinguishes sending a command from receiving a cluster echo.
- Operate a connected radio using PTT over the map in the new-QSO entry screen. Standalone portable logging does not need a radio.

## Personalisation and diagnostics

Portrait and landscape layouts, colour themes, selectable display controls, keep-screen-on, adjustable CW/FT/logbook text sizes, and diagnostic exports for investigating audio, transport and digital-mode behaviour.

## Getting started

1. Install the APK and enter your callsign, locator and region in Settings.
2. Choose **Local**, **SmartLink** or **Logbook** on the opening screen.
3. Select the intended radio, band, mode and antennas. Check audio output and microphone permissions before operating.
4. Configure QRZ and DX-cluster services if you wish to use them.

Android 8.0/API 26 is the declared minimum. Platform microphone Opus support and other device features may impose additional practical requirements. This is an independently developed application, not an official FlexRadio product.
